## Information we collect

- Account information, such as name, email address, phone number, birthday,
  sign-in identifiers, communication preferences, and profile image.
- Order information, such as selected items, customizations, pickup location,
  fulfillment details, loyalty activity, gift-card activity, and order history.
- Payment status and transaction references. Payment processors handle full
  card details; we do not store full payment-card numbers in the app platform.
- Device and usage information, such as push-notification tokens, app version,
  crash diagnostics, security events, and interactions needed to operate and
  improve the service.
- Information you choose to send through messages, reviews, support requests,
  referrals, or preference forms.

## How we use information

We use personal information to authenticate accounts, prepare and fulfill
orders, process and reconcile payments, operate loyalty and gift-card programs,
send requested service or marketing messages, provide support, prevent abuse,
measure service performance, and meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations.

## How we disclose information

We disclose information only as needed to operate the service: to the shop and
its authorized staff; hosting, database, authentication, payment, messaging,
analytics, and error-monitoring providers; professional advisers; authorities
when legally required; and a successor involved in a merger, financing, or sale.
Providers may use information only to deliver their contracted services.

We do not sell personal information. If applicable law defines targeted
advertising or certain analytics disclosures as a "sale" or "sharing," describe

## Retention and account deletion

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above and the
retention periods required by law. You can delete your account in the app from
More → Profile → Delete account. Deletion removes the sign-in identity, contact
details, and push-notification tokens. Order records remain in anonymized form
when the shop must retain transaction history for accounting, tax, fraud, or
legal obligations.
