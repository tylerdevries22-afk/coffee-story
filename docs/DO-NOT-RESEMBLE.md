# DO-NOT-RESEMBLE

The design system is original. This file is the standard the
`audit-originality` skill enforces, written without naming anyone: the rule
is about *kinds* of resemblance, and it applies to every competitor equally,
including ones that don't exist yet.

## Hard rules

1. **No competitor's name appears anywhere** — code, comments, commit
   messages, copy, assets, listings, prompts. The category this platform
   serves is called the **"rotating-drop model."**
2. **Never ingest a competitor's assets, CSS, JS, or screenshots as a spec.**
   Not as a reference image in a generation prompt, not as a color-picked
   palette, not as a traced layout. Inspiration flows from the *tenant's own
   brand material* (their logo, photography, signage, social presence) and
   from first principles.
3. **No verbatim identity matches.** A tenant palette may not reproduce a
   known ordering product's signature color pairing; exact hex matches with a
   competitor's published brand colors fail the audit outright.
4. **No signature compositions.** Generic commerce patterns are fine — a
   sectioned menu, an item sheet with options, a receipt with tax rows, a
   three-column kitchen board. What fails is a screen a reasonable person
   would mistake for a specific other product: their exact hero rhythm, their
   badge and mascot shapes, their distinctive motion signature.
5. **No borrowed voice.** Slogans, catchphrases, points-currency names, and
   distinctive microcopy belong to whoever coined them. Each tenant's
   dictionary comes from their own voice.

## What is deliberately fine

- Table-stakes flows in their conventional order (browse → customize → bag →
  checkout → track). Convention is not trade dress.
- Standard platform idioms (tab bars, bottom sheets, pull to refresh).
- Competing on the same *ideas* — scheduled limited runs, countdowns,
  points — expressed in original form.

## Process

- Derivations are recorded: every tenant palette and type pairing notes what
  brand material it came from (the onboarding skill writes this down).
- The audit runs before any listing, pitch, or public screenshot, and after
  any theming change. Its verdict is binary and it blocks on failure.
