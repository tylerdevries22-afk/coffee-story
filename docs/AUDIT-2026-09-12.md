## 5. Verified clean — do not re-audit

Recorded so the next pass does not repeat the work.

- **RLS coverage:** every `public` table has RLS enabled (148 created, 0 missing); apparent gaps are `app`-schema tables with `revoke all`.
- **Definer hygiene:** all 335 `SECURITY DEFINER` functions pin `search_path` (the `SET search_path TO ''` spelling defeats a naive grep).
- **No policy gated on `jwt_brand_id()` alone**, which is what makes the self-serve `brand_slug` bootstrap claim harmless.
- **Square OAuth tokens** are ciphertext in a table with one service-role policy and appear in no view or definer projection; `resolve_connector_secret` is `service_role` only.
- **Device tokens cannot impersonate staff** (`verifyDeviceToken` rejects `sub` and any `app_metadata.role`); revocation bites on the direct PostgREST path via `app.device_is_active`.
- **`DEMO_SESSION.userId` is `null`** and both `authorizeWorkspaceMutation` and `claimsForWorkspaceMutation` refuse on exactly that, independently — the demo session is structurally incapable of a trusted write.
- **Every service-role path in the console** is closed in demo mode by one of three mechanisms: `serverClient()` null, the null `userId`, or bearer auth.
- **Migration chain:** no duplicate or out-of-order timestamps (enforced by `packages/schema/src/invariants.test.ts`); every `register_release` stamp matches its filename; `pnpm db:local` applies all 186 end to end; `deep-health.test.ts` pins the constant to the newest migration.
- **Hosted promotion** wraps each migration in its own transaction with an idempotency key; a mid-apply network failure cannot double-apply.
- **Money paths:** payment capture, checkout link, webhook settlement and fee quote all cross-validate against durable quotes with exact-match checks and reject rather than guess.
- **a11y-state rule:** every raw `accessibilityState` is paired with its `aria-*` prop; every literal `AppIcon` name resolves.
- **Rule 7:** no operator/admin trees remain in `apps/customer/src` — `docs/AUDIT.md`'s note to the contrary is stale.
- **Display board** reads only the narrow `board_tickets` view — no PII or cart on the public screen.
- **Applied tenant slots** are byte-identical to their `tenants/*` sources for all three applied tenants, both apps.
- **Go-live lock:** nothing was ever minted without approval — the single caller of `mintProductionHosts` sits inside `if (goLiveApproved)`. All five explicit guards were inert; they now carry the real flag and `mintProductionHosts` refuses before its first `await`.
